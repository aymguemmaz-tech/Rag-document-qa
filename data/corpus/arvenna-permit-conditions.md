# City of Arvenna Operating Permit - Conditions

The City of Arvenna granted Veloria Mobility a shared micromobility permit for e-scooters and e-bikes. This summary lists the conditions operations must respect.

## Validity and fees

The permit is valid from 1 April 2025 until 31 March 2028. Veloria pays a permit fee of €65 per vehicle per year, invoiced quarterly by the city.

## Fleet size

The permit allows at most 6,500 vehicles citywide. Any increase above this cap requires a written amendment from the Mobility Department.

## Speed limits

E-scooters and e-bikes are limited to 6 km/h in pedestrian zones and to 15 km/h in the Old Town. Both limits are enforced automatically through geofencing.

## Parking

Vehicles may only be parked in the designated zones published by the city. Misparked vehicles reported by the city must be removed within the times agreed in the City Partner API Guide. A penalty of €50 is charged for every misparked vehicle that is not removed in time.

## Reporting

Veloria shares real-time availability through the public GBFS feed and sends a monthly report with trip volumes, incidents and complaints to the Mobility Department by the tenth day of the following month.

## Equity commitments

At least 15% of the fleet must be deployed every morning in the outer districts of Arvenna.
