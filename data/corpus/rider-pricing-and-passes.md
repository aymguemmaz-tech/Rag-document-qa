# Rider Pricing and Passes

This document defines the prices Veloria Mobility charges riders in Arvenna. It is effective from 1 March 2026 and replaces the 2025 price list. All prices include VAT.

## Pay-as-you-go rides

Every ride starts with an unlock fee of €1.00, whatever the vehicle type. After unlocking, riders pay per minute: €0.25 per minute on an e-scooter and €0.20 per minute on an e-bike. A night surcharge of €0.10 per minute applies to rides between 23:00 and 05:00.

When a ride starts, the app places a pre-authorisation hold of €5 on the payment method. The hold is released within three business days if the final ride amount is charged separately.

## Passes

Passes are bought in the app and renew automatically until cancelled.

| Pass | Price | What is included |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day Pass | €9.50 | Unlimited rides of up to 45 minutes each for 24 hours |
| Monthly Flex Pass | €24.90 | Unlimited unlocks and the first 30 minutes of every ride free |
| Annual Pass | €219 | Monthly Flex benefits for 12 months |

Minutes beyond the included time are billed at the standard per-minute rate. The night surcharge still applies to pass holders.

Students receive a 30% discount on the Monthly Flex Pass, which brings the price to €17.43 per month. The discount requires a valid student card, verified once per academic year in the app.

## Parking fees

Rides must end inside a designated parking zone shown in the app. Ending a ride outside a parking zone costs a €15 parking fee. Ending a ride inside a red no-parking zone costs €35, and the vehicle is relocated by our field team.

Since 1 May 2026 riders must take a photo of the parked vehicle before the ride can end.

## Refunds

Rides interrupted by a technical fault are refunded automatically within 5 business days. Riders can file any other refund claim through in-app support within 14 days of the ride.

## Payment methods

We accept debit and credit cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay. Riders can also top up a prepaid wallet with €10, €20 or €50. A top-up of €50 receives a 5% bonus credit.

## Eligibility

Riders must be at least 18 years old to ride an e-scooter. E-bikes can be ridden from the age of 16 with parental consent recorded in the app.
