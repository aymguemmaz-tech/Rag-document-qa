# Field Technician Handbook

Welcome to the field team. This handbook covers how we work on the streets of Arvenna and what we expect from every technician.

## Shifts

| Shift | Hours | Main tasks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day | 06:00–14:30 | L1 checks, rebalancing |
| Evening | 14:00–22:30 | L1 checks, misparking removals |
| Night | 22:00–06:30 | Battery swaps |

The night shift target is 45 battery swaps per technician. Day and evening shifts aim for 25 rebalancing moves per technician.

## Onboarding and training

New technicians complete a 5-day onboarding programme. It includes a one-day battery safety certification called BAT-SAFE, which must be renewed every 12 months. Nobody may handle damaged battery packs without a valid BAT-SAFE certificate.

## Lone working

Technicians working alone check in through the Ops app every 2 hours. If a check-in is missed, the dispatcher calls the technician after 15 minutes. If the technician cannot be reached within 30 minutes, the dispatcher escalates to the shift supervisor.

## Service vans

Drive service vans at a maximum of 50 km/h in the city, even where higher limits apply. Never leave a van running unattended. Complete the daily van check in the Ops app before your first job.

## Weather

Field work is paused when wind gusts exceed 75 km/h or when a thunderstorm warning is in force.

## Overtime

Overtime must be approved in advance by the shift supervisor. Approved overtime is paid at 150% of the hourly rate.

## Injuries and incidents

Report any injury, however minor, within 24 hours using the incident form in the Ops app.
