# Operations Report Q1 2026

This report covers January to March 2026.

## Headline numbers

Riders took 902,000 trips in Q1 2026, despite a harsh February. On average 84,000 riders were active each month. The average trip lasted 10.8 minutes and covered 2.4 km.

## Fleet

At the end of March the fleet comprised 3,900 e-scooters and 1,600 e-bikes, 5,500 vehicles in total. Fleet availability was 90.4%, below the 92% target, mainly because of cold-weather battery faults. Technicians completed 168,000 battery swaps during the quarter.

## Winter storm

Service was suspended citywide for two days, on 11 and 12 February 2026, because of a winter storm. All vehicles were collected before the storm and redeployed within 36 hours after the city lifted its warning.

## Safety

We recorded 37 safety incidents, 2 of which involved an injury that required medical treatment. That is 4.1 incidents per 100,000 trips.

## Commercial

Revenue for the quarter was €3.31 million. At the end of March 15,800 riders held a Monthly Flex Pass. Customer satisfaction (CSAT) averaged 4.2 out of 5. The new price list took effect on 1 March 2026.

## Priorities for Q2 2026

The main priorities for Q2 were the Eastport launch, the parking-photo requirement and raising fleet availability above target.
