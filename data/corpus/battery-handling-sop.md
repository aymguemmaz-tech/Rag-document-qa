# SOP-BAT-07: Battery Handling and Storage

This standard operating procedure applies to every person who handles Veloria vehicle batteries: field technicians, charging-room staff and depot logistics. Version 3.2 was approved by the Fleet Energy lead in January 2026.

## Battery types

The Veloria S3 scooter uses a 48 V, 15 Ah lithium-ion pack (720 Wh) that weighs 4.2 kg. The Veloria B2 e-bike uses a 36 V, 14 Ah pack (504 Wh). Both packs are hot-swappable and carry a QR code that links to their charge history.

## Storage

Store packs in a dry, ventilated room at 10 °C to 25 °C. Packs must never be stored below 0 °C or above 35 °C. Packs that will be stored for longer than 7 days must be kept at 40–60% state of charge to slow down ageing.

Charging takes place only in fire-rated charging cabinets (EI 90). A single charging cabinet may charge at most 30 packs at the same time.

## Swapping packs in the field

The vehicle platform flags any pack below 15% state of charge for a swap. Before inserting a fresh pack, the technician inspects it for dents, swelling and damaged connectors. The target time for a complete swap is 3 minutes.

## Damaged packs

A pack is considered damaged if it is swollen, punctured or leaking, or if its surface temperature exceeds 60 °C. A damaged pack must be placed in a sand-filled quarantine container within 10 minutes of discovery and marked with a red tag. Damaged packs are never transported in a passenger vehicle. The technician notifies the Fleet Energy lead through the Ops app.

## Fire response

If a pack catches fire, evacuate the area and call 112. Use the AVD (aqueous vermiculite dispersion) extinguisher kept next to every charging cabinet. Never use a CO2 extinguisher on a burning lithium-ion pack, because it does not stop thermal runaway.

## Transport

Service vans may carry at most 40 packs, secured in ventilated racks. Every van that carries packs displays the UN3480 lithium battery label.

## Personal protective equipment

Wear cut-resistant gloves and safety glasses whenever you open a battery compartment.

## End of life

A pack whose state of health falls below 70% of its original capacity is retired. Retired packs are collected by our certified recycling partner.
