# Veloria B2 E-bike Rider Guide

The Veloria B2 is a pedal-assist e-bike designed for city trips. This guide explains how to ride it safely.

## Before you ride

Check the brakes and the tyres, and adjust the saddle so your leg is almost straight at the bottom of the pedal stroke. The lights switch on automatically when the ride starts.

## Pedal assistance

The B2 has three assistance levels: Eco, Tour and Boost. Change the level with the plus and minus buttons on the left handlebar. Assistance stops at 25 km/h, as the law requires.

## Range and battery

With a fully charged pack the B2 can travel up to 70 km in Eco mode. The battery level is shown on the handlebar display and in the app.

## Load limits

The maximum rider weight is 120 kg. The front basket can carry up to 5 kg. Child seats and trailers are not allowed.

## Ending your ride

Park in a designated parking zone, lower the kickstand, lock the rear wheel lock and take the parking photo in the app.

## Problems on the road

If the bike feels unsafe, end the ride, report the problem in the app and do not ride further.
