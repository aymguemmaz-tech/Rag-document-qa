# Data Privacy and Retention Policy

Veloria Mobility processes personal data in line with the GDPR. Questions about this policy go to the Data Protection Officer at privacy@veloria.example.

## What we collect

While a ride is active, vehicles report their location every 10 seconds. When a vehicle is parked, it reports its location every 15 minutes. We also store account details, payment records and support conversations.

## Retention periods

| Data | Retention |
| --- | --- |
| GPS trip traces (identifiable) | 90 days |
| Anonymised trip data | 3 years |
| Payment records | 7 years |
| Support tickets | 2 years after closure |

After 90 days, GPS trip traces are anonymised: the rider ID is removed and locations are rounded to a 100-metre grid. Anonymised trip data is kept for 3 years for urban planning. Payment records are kept for 7 years because accounting law requires it.

## Legal bases

We process trip data to perform the rental contract. Fraud detection relies on our legitimate interest. Marketing messages are only sent with the rider's consent.

## Sharing with cities

City partners receive only aggregated trip data. Every aggregate must contain at least 10 trips, so that no individual journey can be identified.

## Rider rights

Riders can request access to or erasure of their data in the app. We answer data subject requests within 30 days. For complex requests this period can be extended by a further 60 days, and the rider is told why.

## Security and breaches

All personal data is stored in the EU, in our cloud provider's Frankfurt region. If a personal data breach occurs, the supervisory authority is notified within 72 hours.
