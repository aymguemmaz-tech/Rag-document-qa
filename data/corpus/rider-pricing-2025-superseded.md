# Rider Pricing 2025 (superseded)

This price list applied from 1 January 2025 until 28 February 2026. It has been replaced by the current Rider Pricing and Passes document.

## Pay-as-you-go rides

Every ride started with an unlock fee of €0.90. Riders paid €0.22 per minute on an e-scooter and €0.18 per minute on an e-bike. There was no night surcharge.

## Passes

| Pass | Price | What is included |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day Pass | €8.00 | Unlimited rides of up to 30 minutes each for 24 hours |
| Monthly Pass | €19.90 | Unlimited unlocks and the first 20 minutes of every ride free |

## Parking fees

Ending a ride outside a parking zone cost a €10 parking fee.

## Refunds

Riders could file refund claims through in-app support within 7 days of the ride.
