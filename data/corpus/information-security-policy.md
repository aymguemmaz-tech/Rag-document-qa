# Information Security Policy

This policy applies to all employees, contractors and systems of Veloria Mobility.

## Accounts and authentication

Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for every staff account, including email, the Ops app and cloud consoles. Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Shared accounts are not allowed.

## Devices

Company laptops use full-disk encryption and lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the Security team immediately.

## Production access

Engineers access production through single sign-on with just-in-time access requests. Granted production access expires automatically after 8 hours. Access rights are reviewed every quarter by each team lead.

## Vulnerability management

Security patches must be applied within 72 hours for critical vulnerabilities, within 14 days for high-severity vulnerabilities and within 30 days for medium-severity vulnerabilities. An external firm performs a penetration test of the rider app and the partner API once a year.

## Reporting security incidents

Report suspected security incidents to security@veloria.example within 1 hour of noticing them. Do not try to investigate on your own and do not delete evidence.

## Suppliers

Suppliers that process rider data must pass a security assessment before the contract is signed and every two years after that.
