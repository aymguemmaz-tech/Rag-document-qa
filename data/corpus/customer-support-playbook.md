# Customer Support Playbook

This playbook tells support agents how to help riders quickly and consistently.

## Channels and response times

Riders contact us through in-app chat, email and the phone hotline. In-app chat is staffed from 07:00 to 23:00, and our target is a first response within 2 minutes. Emails receive a first response within 24 hours. The phone hotline is reserved for accidents and safety issues and is staffed around the clock.

## Refund decisions

Agents may approve refunds of up to €20 per ride without asking a supervisor. Larger refunds, or more than three refunds for the same rider in a month, need approval from a Tier 2 supervisor. Refunds go back to the original payment method.

## Escalation tiers

Tier 1 agents handle account, payment and ride questions. Tier 2 supervisors handle disputes, fraud suspicions and complaints about staff. Anything involving an injury is escalated immediately to the Safety Officer.

## Lost and found

Items left on vehicles are brought to Depot North by the field team. Lost items are kept for 30 days. After 30 days, unclaimed items are donated to charity or disposed of securely.

## Tone and quality

Agents use the rider's first name, avoid internal jargon and confirm every resolution in writing. After each conversation the rider receives a one-question satisfaction survey.

## Accounts

Agents can block an account for suspected fraud for up to 72 hours while Tier 2 investigates. Only Tier 2 may close an account permanently.
