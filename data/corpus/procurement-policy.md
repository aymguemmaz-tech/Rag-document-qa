# Procurement Policy

This policy defines who may approve purchases and how suppliers are selected.

## Approval thresholds

| Purchase value | Approver | Quotes required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Up to €5,000 | Team lead | 1 |
| €5,000 to €50,000 | Department head | 2 |
| Above €50,000 | Chief Financial Officer | 3 |

Purchases must not be split into smaller orders to avoid a threshold.

## Vehicles and spare parts

New vehicles are bought under the framework agreement with Kinetix Manufacturing, which runs until the end of 2027. Spare parts are ordered through the workshop's inventory system, which raises purchase orders automatically when stock falls below the minimum level.

## Supplier selection

Suppliers are scored on price, quality, delivery reliability and sustainability. Sustainability counts for 20% of the score. New suppliers must provide proof of insurance and agree to the supplier code of conduct.

## Payment terms

Invoices are paid within 30 days of receipt unless a contract states otherwise.

## Conflicts of interest

Employees must declare any personal relationship with a supplier before taking part in a selection.
