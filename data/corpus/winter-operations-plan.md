# Winter Operations Plan 2026/27

Winter readiness is a Q3 2026 priority. This plan explains how we keep riders and vehicles safe between November and March.

## Fleet size

From December to February we operate a reduced winter fleet of 3,500 vehicles. Vehicles taken out of service are stored, serviced and given an L3 overhaul before spring.

## Tyres

Winter tyres are fitted to all e-bikes from 1 November. E-scooters keep their standard tyres because no approved winter tyre exists for the Veloria S3.

## Weather rules

E-scooters are withdrawn from service when the road surface temperature falls below −2 °C or when snow covers the road. E-bikes stay available unless the city issues a storm warning.

## Batteries in cold weather

When the night temperature is forecast below 0 °C, all spare packs are moved indoors before 20:00. Packs that have been outside below 0 °C must warm up for at least 2 hours before charging.

## Storm procedure

When the city issues a storm warning, all vehicles are collected within 6 hours and service is suspended. The February 2026 storm showed that redeployment after a storm takes about 36 hours.

## Staffing

The night battery team is reduced by one third during the winter, while two extra day technicians are scheduled to clear snow from parking zones.
