# Operations Report Q2 2026

This report covers April to June 2026.

## Headline numbers

Riders took 1,284,000 trips in Q2 2026, up 42% from 902,000 trips in Q1 2026. On average 118,500 riders were active each month. The average trip lasted 11.4 minutes and covered 2.6 km.

## Fleet

At the end of June the fleet comprised 4,200 e-scooters and 1,800 e-bikes, 6,000 vehicles in total. Fleet availability reached 93.1%, above the 92% target. Technicians completed 212,000 battery swaps during the quarter.

## Safety

We recorded 41 safety incidents, 3 of which involved an injury that required medical treatment. That is 3.2 incidents per 100,000 trips.

## Commercial

Revenue for the quarter was €4.87 million, or €8.92 per vehicle per day. At the end of June 21,300 riders held a Monthly Flex Pass. Customer satisfaction (CSAT) averaged 4.4 out of 5.

## Parking

Misparking complaints fell by 18% after riders were required to photograph their parked vehicle from 1 May.

## Expansion

We launched the Eastport district on 2 June 2026 with 400 vehicles.

## Priorities for Q3 2026

The main priorities for Q3 are winter readiness of the fleet and growing Eastport to 700 vehicles.
