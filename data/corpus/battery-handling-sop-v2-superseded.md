# SOP-BAT-07 v2.4: Battery Handling (superseded)

This version of the battery procedure was replaced by version 3.2 in January 2026. It is kept for audit purposes only.

## Storage

Store packs at 5 °C to 30 °C in a dry room. For long-term storage, keep packs at 30–50% state of charge.

## Charging

A single charging cabinet may charge at most 20 packs at the same time.

## Damaged packs

Place a damaged pack in a metal quarantine box within 15 minutes of discovery.

## Transport

Service vans may carry at most 30 packs.
